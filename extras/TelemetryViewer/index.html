Getting started

  1. Flash the Dongle example to an ESP32 on this computer (for full speed, an S2 or S3 with USB CDC On Boot enabled).
  2. On the robot: Telemetry.begin("MyRobot"); in setup(), and Telemetry.add("name", value); wherever you like.
  3. Click Connect and pick the dongle's port, then pick your robot and click Pair. After that, the page pairs with that robot again on its own.
Click a plot to select it, then click channels on the right to add or remove them. Drag across a plot to zoom; double-click to go back.
